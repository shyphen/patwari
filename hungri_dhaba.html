25 YEARS OF PUNJABI PRIDE

Hungri Dhaba was founded in 1998 by Sardar Gurpreet Singh in the heart of Model Town, Ludhiana. What started as a small roadside stall with three tables has grown into Ludhiana's most loved traditional eatery — without ever changing the recipes.

Today, Sardar Ji's son Navjot runs the kitchen, but the masalas are still ground fresh each morning, the dahi is still set in earthen pots, and every guest still leaves feeling like family.
